# BBA Basketball Jersey Guidelines

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Adopted baseline |
| Decision date | 7 August 2026 |
| Owner | Director |
| Scope | Basketball jerseys supplied for Bendigo Basketball Association competition |

## 1. Authority decision

Pivot adopts the **Bendigo Basketball Association General By-Laws, September 2021, section 20 — Player Uniforms** as its controlling BBA jersey baseline until BBA publishes a replacement.

Written confirmation from BBA is not a dependency. The detailed 2021 by-laws remain the best available published source; BBA's current Forms & Information page does not publish a newer detailed replacement. The 2023 Basic BBA Rules for Beginners confirms that players should be in uniform and that uniform penalties apply, but does not replace the detailed jersey requirements.

Review this baseline when BBA publishes replacement competition or uniform rules. Do not silently replace or reinterpret it.

## 2. Relationship to FIBA

BBA adopts FIBA rules with local modifications. Its jersey-number requirements meet or exceed the corresponding FIBA 2024 and FIBA 2026 dimensions:

| Requirement | Adopted BBA baseline | FIBA 2024 and 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Front number height | At least 10 cm | At least 8 cm |
| Back number height | At least 20 cm | At least 16 cm |
| Number width | At least 2 cm | At least 2 cm |
| Logo and advertising clearance | At least 5 cm | At least 4 cm |

FIBA 2026 becomes valid on 1 October 2026 and retains the same corresponding number and clearance requirements as FIBA 2024.

## 3. Jersey requirements

Each team jersey must satisfy all of the following:

- team tops use the same colour and pattern;
- the player number appears on the front and back;
- numbers are plain, solid-coloured, contrasting with the jersey, clearly visible and legible;
- the front number is at least 10 cm high;
- the back number is at least 20 cm high;
- the number is at least 2 cm wide;
- every logo and advertisement is at least 5 cm from the numbers;
- numbers used by players in the same team are unique; and
- the jersey provides an alternative colour for a colour clash.

The BBA wording is “numbers shall be at least 2cm wide.” This baseline records that wording as **minimum number width**. It does not reinterpret it as minimum stroke width.

BBA permits `0`, `00` and `1–99`. This is the competition's maximum permitted set, not a requirement that every club allow every value. Any narrower club policy is captured for the relevant jersey in the Product Workflow and cannot broaden the competition set.

Incorrect uniform may attract a five-point penalty per player, capped at 15 points for the team under the adopted BBA by-laws.

## 4. Reversible-jersey product baseline

Pivot will satisfy the practical two-colour requirement with **one reversible jersey**, so families are not required to purchase two separate shirts.

- One face is light-coloured, preferably white.
- One face is dark-coloured.
- The faces must be clearly distinguishable for colour-clash handling.
- Both faces independently satisfy every number, contrast, legibility and clearance requirement in section 3.
- The same player number appears on the front and back of both faces.

This is Pivot's BBA jersey product baseline. It does not pre-approve a Phoenix design, colours, artwork, sponsor, logo placement, supplier, material, cut or manufacturing template.

## 5. Product and production boundaries

The Product Workflow captures product-specific decisions, including any club restriction on permitted numbers, exact colours, artwork, sponsorship and placements. Club Onboarding does not own those jersey decisions.

Final physical measurements and clearances must be checked on the selected supplier's approved reversible-jersey production template. Supplier validation does not replace the BBA baseline, club approval or Pivot's independent production approval.

Current Design Studio artwork, colours and logo placements are placeholders unless an exact product version receives the required approvals. In particular, this guideline grants no approval for Pivot logo placement on a Phoenix jersey.
