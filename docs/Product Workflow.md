# Product workflow

**Status:** Design required — workflow steps have not been agreed

**Owner:** Pivot Teamwear

This page is the destination from the **Club onboarding journey**. The Product Workflow must be designed as a separate process before implementation.

## Agreed boundaries only

- The Product Workflow becomes available after Club Onboarding is complete.
- The Club Administrator and active Club Users participate according to their roles.
- The commercial terms authorise the Club Administrator to approve products and accept related quotes under the standing agreement.
- The workflow must include the required Club Administrator and Pivot approvals.
- Its relevant output to the Club onboarding journey is **Product approved for Store**.
- An approved product is added to the Club Store automatically.
- The first approved product makes a private Store ready for the separate initial Club Store publication process.
- Future approved products are added to an already published Store without repeating initial Store publication approval.

No internal product-workflow steps are approved or specified by this document.
