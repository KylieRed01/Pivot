## 1. Purpose

This Constitution establishes the non-negotiable ground rules for how Pivot Teamwear operates, makes decisions, serves customers, uses technology and grows.

It applies across the whole business, including commercial activity, operations, brand, suppliers, fulfilment, technology and customer service.

## 2. Authority and Governance

- The Director has final authority to approve, amend or waive this Constitution.
- Any amendment or waiver must be recorded with its version, date, owner and reason.
- A waiver must state its scope, risk and expiry date.
- Pivot must comply with applicable law and its approved governing documents.

## 3. Governing Documents

Pivot's governing documents are, in order of precedence:

1. Business Plan
2. Operating Model
3. Brand Kit

Where these documents conflict, Pivot must correct the conflict. It must not silently choose one document or operate under inconsistent rules.

The Constitution records the ground rules derived from these documents and approved Director decisions. It does not replace the need to maintain the governing documents.

## 4. Business Purpose and Customer Commitment

Pivot exists to make quality teamwear accessible to community sport.

Pivot will:

- provide quality custom teamwear through fair pricing, reliable local service and a simple customer experience;
- reduce unnecessary administration for clubs and their members;
- operate professionally, clearly, practically and respectfully;
- maintain affordable quality without compromising promised product quality or customer experience; and
- support community sport throughout Greater Bendigo through trusted local relationships.

## 5. Operating Principles

Pivot will operate according to these principles:

- **Customer first:** make decisions that improve the club and member experience.
- **Affordable quality:** provide genuine value without compromising quality or service.
- **Local service:** provide responsive, reliable support and fulfilment in Greater Bendigo.
- **Simple, repeatable processes:** standardise work, reduce unnecessary complexity and document how work is done.
- **Human oversight where it matters:** automate repeatable, rules-based work while retaining human responsibility for quality assurance, exceptions and customer support.
- **Continuous improvement:** use operational experience and customer feedback to improve processes, products and service.

## 6. Product and Customer Controls

- Pivot will use approved garment products and supplier-validated production geometry rather than unrestricted bespoke garment construction.
- Customers may create fully customisable visual designs within that controlled physical framework, without predefined designs or fixed artwork slots. Designs remain subject to applicable manufacturing, product, sport, competition, brand, legal and approval requirements, and Pivot retains product, artwork and quality control.
- A club must approve its club decisions through its authorised administrator.
- Pivot independently reviews designs and controls publication and release to manufacture; club approval alone does not release work to manufacture.
- A manufacturer may produce only from information authorised by Pivot.
- Pivot is responsible for the customer fulfilment experience, including quality assurance before products are provided to customers.
- Club administrators may access only their own club's administration and data.
- Pivot retains oversight and override access across all clubs.
- Customers may browse and buy from any club store when transactional stores are introduced.

## 7. Pilot and Growth Discipline

- Phoenix United Basketball Development Club is the controlled pilot customer.
- No marketing or sales activity will occur outside Phoenix until the pilot demonstrates a repeatable and commercially viable model.
- Expansion beyond Phoenix requires the pilot success measures in the Business Plan to be met.
- The Director may approve a documented exception only where the unmet measure, risk and expiry date are recorded.
- Pivot will grow through controlled expansion, operational readiness and continuous improvement, not rapid scaling.
- Product-range expansion, additional clubs and new operating models must not compromise quality, fulfilment control or the customer experience.

## 8. Technology Principles

- Technology is an enabler of Pivot's operating model, not a substitute for operational discipline.
- Technology must simplify customer interactions, reduce manual administration and improve reliability, consistency and scalability.
- Pivot will build a multi-club foundation while restricting production pilot access to Phoenix.
- The design experience is a Pivot-owned capability. Established components may be used where they reduce delivery risk without surrendering Pivot's customer, design or approval records.
- Customer data, uploaded artwork, database backups and audit records under Pivot's control must be stored and processed in Australia in production. Global delivery may be used only for static public assets. After formal Pivot release, an authorised manufacturer may receive only the minimum immutable package required to manufacture the released work; this exception grants no broader access to Pivot's platform or records.
- Club administrator access will use passwordless email sign-in. Pivot administrator access requires multi-factor authentication.
- Pivot must retain an audit record of design changes, store publication and approvals, including actor, version and timestamp.
- Approval records must be retained for the life of the related product and order, plus seven years.

## 9. Brand and Communications

- All Pivot communications and touchpoints must follow the Brand Kit.
- Communications must be clear, professional, approachable, practical and positive.
- Pivot must avoid jargon, exaggerated claims, ambiguous language, unnecessary technical language and unprofessional slang.
- Pivot must present itself consistently across stores, documents, products, digital channels and customer service.

## 10. Review

The Director will review this Constitution when Pivot's operating model materially changes, at the conclusion of the Phoenix pilot, and before broader expansion.
