## Domain 4 — Public trial and persistence

- Visitors can try without joining Pivot or providing contact details.
- No mandatory tutorial, popup or chatbot.
- Public state/artwork stays browser-local and is never uploaded or recoverable by Pivot.
- Public visitors cannot permanently save, share, submit or approve.

- Public experimentation never establishes official club colours or approved assets.

## Domain 5 — Editor model

- Structured, view-independent design state.
- Four authoritative 2D surfaces: dark/light × front/back.
- Reversible faces are linked but independently refinable and ultimately approved/released together.
- Reliable 3D is preferred, not a pilot blocker; current 3D is indicative only.
- Flexible, template-constrained composition rather than fixed logo slots.
- Template metadata defines Fixed, Constrained and Flexible elements.
- Draft experimentation may be non-compliant; blocking errors prevent submission.
- Supplier-impossible operations remain unavailable once authoritative geometry exists.
- Zoom, fit, reset, pan and accessible non-drag alternatives remain baseline navigation.
- WCAG 2.2 AA remains the formal customer-facing target.

**Verification:** APPROVE / CHANGE

## Domain 6 — Colours, artwork, text, numbers and patterns

- Direct colour selection/confirmation is the reliable baseline; technical colour knowledge is optional.
- Automatic extraction is deterministic optional assistance, not an approval source or pilot blocker.
- Requested club colours and production mappings are separate.
- Public visitors may use ordinary artwork but are not offered completed-garment conversion.
- Authenticated completed-design onboarding may require Pivot assistance.
- Ordinary artwork receives flexible controls; official logos remain protected and versioned.
- Background removal is user-invoked, non-destructive and preferred, not blocking.
- Baseline text controls remain those locked in the security handover; supplier-dependent fonts/outlines remain unresolved.
- Basketball numbers are required and cannot be removed; representative styling is separate from the production number run.
- Basketball player names are unavailable in Phase 1.
- Pattern freedom remains subject to supplier capability and required-element visibility.

**Verification:** APPROVE / CHANGE

## Domain 7 — Roles, approval, proof and manufacture

- Up to two club users and one appointed administrator.
- Club users prepare; administrator formally approves; Pivot independently reviews technical/production/compliance/quality matters.
- Club and Pivot approval must come from different people.
- A small-club administrator may prepare and club-approve the same work.
- Club user submission precedes administrator approval of an exact immutable version.
- Final 2D proof is mandatory; physical sample is risk-based; 3D is never sole evidence.
- Administrator's final action is **Approve and authorise production**.
- Separate authorised Pivot action is **Release to manufacture**.
- Authorised/sent/supplier-accepted states remain distinct.
- Approvals/history are immutable and never erased.
- Current workflow UI is a simulation only.

**Verification:** APPROVE / CHANGE
