## How are club designs approved?

**CHANGE REQUIRED**

**Approved before**

> Club users submit designs to the administrator for approval. Once the administrator approves the digital design, we check the product, production, sport and competition requirements.
>
> We then arrange a final design proof for production and, where required, a physical sample. We complete our quality checks before reviewing the proof or sample with the club administrator for final approval.
>
> Once approved, the design is ready to be made and added to the club store.

**Proposed minimal replacement**

> Club users submit an exact design version to the administrator for club approval. Pivot then independently checks the product, production, sport and competition requirements.
>
> Pivot arranges the final 2D production proof and, where risk requires it, a physical sample. The administrator reviews the exact proof and gives final production authorisation through a separate confirmation.
>
> An authorised Pivot person then performs a separate release to manufacture. Until supplier and manufacturing inputs are resolved, the demonstrator cannot complete this workflow.

**Reason:** Apply the later exact-version, proof, final-authorisation and separate-release decisions.

**Proposed minimal replacement**

>
> The current demonstrator does not create a live club store.

**Reason:** Preserve the approved setup model while removing a live-store implication.
